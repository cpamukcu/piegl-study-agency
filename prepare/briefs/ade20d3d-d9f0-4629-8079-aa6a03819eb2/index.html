Innovation Management, Wed 4 Nov

AI interpretation by claude-sonnet-5-5, made Today 18:17. Every point links to the page it came from: check it there before you rely on it.

From: Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations

Overview

This class explains how new products and ideas spread through a market. It introduces the five adopter categories, the five attributes that make adoption faster, and applies them to mobile payments in China.

Key points

  1. Adoption follows a bell curve of five adopter categories, from innovators to laggards.(Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 2)
  2. Many products stall in the 'chasm' between early adopters and the early majority.(Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 2)
  3. Relative advantage, compatibility, low complexity, trialability and observability speed adoption.(Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 3)
  4. Mobile payments in China spread fast because network effects and QR codes lowered complexity.(Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 4)

Concepts: mark how well you know each

  • Diffusion of innovations: How a new product or idea spreads through a population over time.(Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 1)

  • Network effects: A product becomes more valuable as more people use it.(Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 4)

  • The chasm: The gap between enthusiastic early adopters and the pragmatic early majority.(Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 2)

  • Early majority: The pragmatic third of adopters who wait for proof that a product works.(Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 2)

  • Trialability: How easily people can try an innovation before committing to it.(Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 3)

Questions you could raise in class

  • Does the chasm apply to B2B products in the same way as consumer products?(Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 2)
  • Which of the five attributes mattered most for mobile payments outside China?(Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 3; Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 4)

Flashcards (4)

  • Name the five adopter categories.

    Innovators, early adopters, early majority, late majority, laggards.(Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 2)

  • What is the chasm?

    The gap between early adopters and the early majority.(Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 2)

  • Five attributes that speed adoption?

    Relative advantage, compatibility, complexity, trialability, observability.(Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 3)

  • Why did QR codes help mobile payments?

    They lowered complexity and made use visible to others.(Week 8 - Diffusion of innovations, p. 4)
