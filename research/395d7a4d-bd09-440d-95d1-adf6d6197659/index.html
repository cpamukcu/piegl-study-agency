How do start-up visa programmes shape where international founders build their companies?

Plan

AI interpretation by claude-sonnet-5-5. Suggestions for how to search, not findings. It names no sources on purpose.

Smaller questions

  • Which countries offer start-up visas, and on what terms?
  • What do founders say drives their choice of country?
  • How do outcomes of visa-based start-ups compare with others?

Search terms

  • start-up visa
  • entrepreneur visa policy
  • founder migration
  • immigrant entrepreneurship
  • 创业签证
  • 人才签证

Perspectives to compare

  • Institutional theory
  • Talent competition between countries
  • Founders' own decision-making

Where to look

  • Google Scholar and Web of Science
  • ZJU library databases, including CNKI
  • Government policy documents
  • OECD migration reports

Steps

  • Define what counts as a start-up visa
  • Collect the policies of 5–8 countries
  • Read 10–15 peer-reviewed studies
  • Compare in a table
  • Write your own argument
